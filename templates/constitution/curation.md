# Curation

The agent admits reflexes under these rules; `lampstand compile` checks every one of them and refuses a house that
breaks one.

- Every new principle, provision or reflex carries an occasion: what happened that calls for it.
- Every principle carries a failure mode.
- Every reflex carries a falsification trigger (`falsified-when`) and a revision step (`revise`).
- Budgets and the ratchet hold: a surface over its cap may not grow.
- No budget total is declared; totals are derived.
- Generated files carry their header and match a fresh compile.
- Every gate a provision names exists.
- At most five surfaces load unconditionally.

Judgements of taste are never errors.
